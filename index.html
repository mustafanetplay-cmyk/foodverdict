Support

Questions, feedback or a product that looks wrong? Email mustafanetplay@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Subscriptions

FoodVerdict Pro is managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase, open FoodVerdict → Profile → Restore Purchases.

Product data

Product information comes from Open Food Facts, a free, community-contributed database. If a product is missing or incorrect, you can add or fix it there.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Summary

FoodVerdict is designed to collect as little data as possible. You don't need an account, and your scans, favorites and goals stay on your device.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. No personal information is sent with this request.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to read barcodes and nutrition labels. Label text recognition happens entirely on your device, and photos are not uploaded or stored by FoodVerdict.

Goals and preferences

Your selected goals and diet preferences are stored locally on your device and are used only to calculate your For You score. FoodVerdict does not collect health data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. FoodVerdict receives only the information needed to confirm your subscription status and never sees your payment details.

Analytics and tracking

FoodVerdict does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can clear your scan history in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Children

FoodVerdict is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at mustafanetplay@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

FoodVerdict provides educational nutrition information and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, dietitian or other qualified professional.

Product data may come from community sources and may contain errors or be out of date. Product formulations change. Always check the product packaging, especially for allergens.

FoodVerdict Scores and For You scores are estimates calculated from available data. They are not official ratings and do not guarantee suitability for any individual.

FoodVerdict Pro is an auto-renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA).

Gizlilik Politikası

FoodVerdict mümkün olan en az veriyi toplayacak şekilde tasarlandı. Hesap gerekmez; taramaların, favorilerin ve hedeflerin cihazında kalır.

Bir barkod taradığında, ürün bilgisini almak için yalnızca barkod numarası Open Food Facts'e gönderilir. Bu istekle hiçbir kişisel bilgi gönderilmez.

Kamera yalnızca barkod ve besin etiketi okumak için kullanılır. Etiket metni tamamen cihazında tanınır; fotoğraflar FoodVerdict tarafından yüklenmez veya saklanmaz.

Seçtiğin hedefler ve beslenme tercihlerin yalnızca cihazında saklanır ve sadece Sana Özel skorunu hesaplamak için kullanılır. FoodVerdict sağlık verisi toplamaz.

Abonelikler Apple tarafından işlenir; FoodVerdict ödeme bilgilerini asla görmez. Üçüncü taraf analiz veya reklam SDK'sı kullanılmaz, takip yapılmaz.

Tarama geçmişini uygulamadan istediğin zaman silebilirsin. Uygulamayı silmek, cihazda saklanan tüm verileri kaldırır.

Kullanım Koşulları

FoodVerdict bilgilendirme amaçlı beslenme bilgisi sunar ve tıbbi tavsiye değildir. Ürün verileri hata içerebilir; özellikle alerjenler için her zaman ambalajı kontrol et. Kullanım ayrıca Apple'ın Standart EULA'sına tabidir.
